What if this page sat under the trees at evening.

Off-white paper, warm sun through leaves, shadows that drift when the wind moves. The site stays quiet. The light does the talking.

A place to read

Imagine opening a note or a portfolio and finding it lit the way a blank wall looks when the sun is low and the canopy is restless — soft, gold-edged, never the same twice.

The shadows aren’t decorations pasted on top. They’re the evening itself, cast across whatever you’re reading.

How it feels

Cream ground. Long, lazy leaf shapes. Slow sway, occasional gust. Multiply-blend so the type stays readable while the ground breathes.

This is a single-file demo: canvas leaf silhouettes + a warm wash. Drop the same layer behind any site chrome and it becomes “under the trees.”
